Have a working key, or lost them all?

You still have a working key

That is a spare key: the cheapest and fastest replacement. At your car we cut the new key and copy or add it to the immobilizer. Your existing key stays programmed and keeps working alongside the new one.

No working key at all

That is an all keys lost job. We cut a new key from your vehicle’s code, program the transponder or smart key to the immobilizer, and leave you with a working key. From $190 for Toyota, Honda, Ford, GM, Hyundai, Kia, Nissan, Jeep and most other makes. Not sure where to start? Read what to do when all car keys are lost.

You already bought a key or fob

That is key programming: we cut and program a key you already have, from $99, or $129 for a push-to-start smart key. Send the part number or a photo before the visit and we check it. If it can’t be programmed to your car (wrong type, used or locked), the trip and check is $75.

How car key replacement works at your car

1. Send the car

Call, text or use the form: year, make and model, whether it starts with a key or a button, and where the car is.

2. Get the price

We check your vehicle’s key system and quote before anyone drives out. The price you hear is the price you pay at the car.

3. The key is cut at the car

We open the car if it’s locked, look up the key code by VIN or read it from a door lock, and cut the key. With a working key, we cut the new one to match it.

4. Programmed and tested

The key is programmed to the immobilizer. If a key was lost, we erase it from the car’s memory, so it can no longer open or start it. Then we test the doors, the ignition and the remote buttons before we leave.

Time on site: most cars take about an hour once we arrive. Some European models take longer, and we tell you that before we drive out.

Warranty: 1 year on keys and remotes we supply. If one stops working within a year, we fix or replace it free. Not covered: physical or water damage, lost keys and batteries. A key you bought elsewhere is tested before you pay but isn’t under warranty.

Locksmith or dealer for a replacement key?

A dealer works on the car at the dealership. If you lost every key, the car has to be towed there first, and some keys are ordered from the factory, which can take days. We do the same cutting and programming at your car with the same manufacturer key data, usually the same day.

For a lost smart key, dealers in the Atlanta area usually end up at $400 to $1,200 once the key, programming and a tow are counted. You can also choose an original key or a quality aftermarket key that costs less. More in the guide: car key from the dealer or a locksmith?

Common questions

How much does car key replacement cost in Cumming, GA?

A spare key with a chip starts at $140, a key with remote or a flip key at $150, a push-to-start smart key at $180 (2020 and newer from $200), and a new key when all keys are lost at $190 for Toyota, Honda, Ford, GM, Hyundai, Kia, Nissan, Jeep and most other makes. Subaru and European makes are quoted by model. Coming to you is included, and the price is quoted before we drive out.

How long does it take to replace a car key?

Most cars take about an hour once we arrive. Some European models take longer, and we tell you that before we drive out.

Can you replace a car key without the original?

Yes. The key is cut from the vehicle’s key code, looked up by VIN or read from a door lock, and then programmed to the car’s immobilizer. No working key is needed.

Do you come to me, or do I bring the car?

We come to you. We bring the key machine, blanks and programmer to wherever the car is: home, work, a parking lot or the side of the road. No tow.

Can you make a car key from the VIN?

For many makes, yes. We’re certified to access manufacturer key data, where the VIN gives the key code. The key is cut from that code and then programmed to the car. When the code isn’t available by VIN, we read it from a door lock with a decoder tool.

Is a locksmith cheaper than the dealer for a replacement key?

Usually, and you skip the tow. For a lost smart key, dealers in the Atlanta area usually end up at $400 to $1,200 once the key, programming and a tow are counted. LocksMitch starts at $190 for all keys lost and $180 for a spare smart key, coming to you included.

Which cars can’t you make a key for?

Mercedes-Benz 2015 and newer: we don’t make those keys, the dealer does. BMW G-series: spare key only while one working key exists. Some brand-new models are not supported by aftermarket tools yet. Text us the year, make and model and we tell you before anyone drives out, for free.

Can you replace a car key at night or on a Sunday?

At night, yes: from 9 pm to 7 am there is a $50 night charge. On Sunday I take only lockouts and all keys lost; a spare key is made Monday to Saturday.

Do you work on push-to-start and smart keys?

Yes, for most makes. Some newer models need extra steps or parts. Tell us the year, make and model and we’ll confirm before coming out.

Can this damage my car?

We take care not to. We open doors with automotive lockout tools, not by force, and program keys with dealer-level equipment, usually through the car’s diagnostic port.

Is there a warranty?

Yes, 1 year on keys and remotes we supply. If one stops working within a year, we fix or replace it free. Not covered: physical or water damage, lost keys and batteries. Programming or cutting a key you bought elsewhere is tested before you pay, but isn’t under warranty, since we can’t vouch for the part. Want the warranty? Get the key from us. No paperwork to keep: the warranty is tied to your phone number and the job date in our records, and we put a LocksMitch tag with our number on your keys.
