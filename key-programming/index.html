How key fob programming works

  1. Check the partSend the year, make, model and the part number or a photo of the key.
  2. Confirm it fitsWe check that the key matches your car before anyone drives out.
  3. Cut if neededBlade keys get cut to your car so they turn in the ignition and doors.
  4. Program and testWe program the key and remote buttons and test everything at the car.

Common questions

Can you program a key I bought on Amazon or eBay?

Often yes, if it is the right part for your car and hasn’t been locked to another vehicle. Send the part number and we’ll check.

How much does key fob programming cost?

From $99 for a key or fob you bought, cutting included. A push-to-start smart key is $129 and a remote without a blade is from $89. If the part can’t be programmed to your car, the trip and check is $75.

Do I have to go to the dealer to program a key fob?

No. We program most makes at your car with dealer-level equipment, usually through the diagnostic port: at home, at work or in a parking lot.

Do you program remotes too?

Yes. Lock, unlock and trunk buttons are programmed along with the key.

What if the key turns out to be wrong for my car?

That’s why we check the part first. If it can’t work with your car, we tell you before coming out.

Is it cheaper than the dealership?

Usually, and you skip the tow. The dealer needs the car on their lot; we do the same programming in your driveway. Send the vehicle details and we’ll quote it.

Can you make a key if I’ve lost every one?

Yes. That’s the “all keys lost” job. We cut the key from your vehicle’s code and program it on site.

Do you work on push-to-start and smart keys?

Yes, for most makes. Some newer models need extra steps or parts. Tell us the year, make and model and we’ll confirm before coming out.

Do I need to tow my car to you?

No. We bring the key machine, blanks and programmer to wherever the car is: home, work, a parking lot or the side of the road.

Can this damage my car?

We take care not to. We open doors with automotive lockout tools, not by force, and program keys with dealer-level equipment, usually through the car’s diagnostic port.

Is there a warranty?

Yes, 1 year on keys and remotes we supply. If one stops working within a year, we fix or replace it free. Not covered: physical or water damage, lost keys and batteries. Programming or cutting a key you bought elsewhere is tested before you pay, but isn’t under warranty, since we can’t vouch for the part. Want the warranty? Get the key from us. No paperwork to keep: the warranty is tied to your phone number and the job date in our records, and we put a LocksMitch tag with our number on your keys.

What are your hours?

We take calls and go out 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays.

How can I pay?

Cash, credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Zelle, Venmo or Cash App, whichever is easiest for you.

Can I text instead of calling?

Yes. Text (770) 270-3333 with the year, make and model and what happened. You’ll get a reply from the locksmith, not a call center.

Is there a discount on a second key?

Yes. When we make a new key because all keys are lost, a second key for the same car is 50% off. Same car, same key type, made during the same visit. Combines with $15 off for online booking.

How do I get the $15 discount?

Book your appointment through the online booking page. The discount is applied to online bookings. Valid on spare keys and all-keys-lost jobs only.
