Privacy policy

Updated October 17, 2026

Short version: we use your details only to help with your car, we never sell them, and the only cookies on this site come from Google Analytics.

LocksMitch is a mobile auto locksmith in North Atlanta, operated by TPVision LLC, Cumming, Georgia. This page explains what information we collect on locksmitch.com and when you contact us, why we collect it, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Estimate requests. The service you need, your vehicle’s year, make and model, your first name and your phone number.
  • Online bookings. The service, the day and time you pick, your vehicle, where the car will be, your first name, your mobile number and any note you add.
  • Calls, texts and messages. Your phone number or messenger account and what you tell us about the job, such as where the car is parked.
  • Site usage. Google Analytics records pages visited, which buttons were tapped (for example “Call” or “Text”), approximate location by city, and device and browser type. It does not tell us your name or phone number.

How we use it

  • To prepare your estimate and to text or call you about your request.
  • To get to your car, do the job and follow up if something needs attention afterwards.
  • To see which pages and buttons help people reach us, so we can improve the site.

We contact you only about your request. We don’t sell, rent or trade your information.

Cookies

The only cookies on locksmitch.com are Google Analytics cookies (named _ga and _ga_XWXQMEEC9Y). They help us count visits and see how the site is used. We don’t use advertising or retargeting cookies.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google’s Analytics opt-out add-on. The site works the same without them.

Who else sees it

Only the services we use to run the site and answer you:

  • Google: Analytics, and the email service that delivers your estimate request to us.
  • Google also hosts our online booking system: when you book a visit, your request is stored in our Google account and we text you from our business phone to confirm.
  • Messaging apps you choose to reach us through, such as WhatsApp, Telegram or Instagram, under their own policies.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep job details as long as we need them for the job, any follow-up or warranty, and our business and tax records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

You can ask what information we have about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it. Email info@locksmitch.com or call or text (770) 270-3333.

Children

This site is not meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update it on this page and change the date at the top.
