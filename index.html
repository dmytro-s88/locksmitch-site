What we do

Four jobs, all done at your car. Most take under an hour once we arrive.

All keys lost

You have no working key at all.

We cut a new key from your vehicle’s code, program the transponder or smart key to the immobilizer, and leave you with a working key. Most makes and models, including push-to-start.

Spare key

You have one key and want a backup.

The cheapest moment to get a second key is while you still have the first. We clone or add a new key or fob so both work.

Locked out of car

Keys are inside, or the lock won’t open.

We open the door without damage to paint, glass or weatherstripping. Call rather than filling the form so we can dispatch right away.

Key programming only

You bought a key or fob and it doesn’t start the car.

We program keys and remotes you already own, as long as the hardware matches your vehicle. Tell us the part number and we’ll confirm before we come.

Common questions

Is it cheaper than the dealership?

Usually, and you skip the tow. The dealer needs the car on their lot; we do the same programming in your driveway. Send the vehicle details and we’ll quote it.

Can you make a key if I’ve lost every one?

Yes. That’s the “all keys lost” job. We cut the key from your vehicle’s code and program it on site.

Do you work on push-to-start and smart keys?

Yes, for most makes. Some newer models need extra steps or parts. Tell us the year, make and model and we’ll confirm before coming out.

How do I get the $15 discount?
