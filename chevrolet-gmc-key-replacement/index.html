Chevrolet and GMC questions

Can you make a Chevrolet and GMC key if I lost all of them?

Yes. We cut a new key from your vehicle’s key code and program it to the car on site. You don’t need the old key. A second key for the same car is 50% off in the same visit.

Do you program Chevrolet and GMC smart keys and push-to-start fobs?

Yes. Smart keys are programmed at your car the same way a dealer does it, without the tow and without the wait.

Original or aftermarket Chevrolet and GMC key?

Your choice. We carry both: original keys from the manufacturer and quality aftermarket keys that cost less. We tell you the price of each before we come out.

Do you work on the newest Chevrolet and GMC models?

Most of them. A few of the very newest models need parts or steps we confirm first. Send the year and model and we tell you upfront, before anyone drives out.

Is it cheaper than the dealership?

Usually, and you skip the tow. The dealer needs the car on their lot; we do the same programming in your driveway. Send the vehicle details and we’ll quote it.

Can you make a key if I’ve lost every one?

Yes. That’s the “all keys lost” job. We cut the key from your vehicle’s code and program it on site.

Do you work on push-to-start and smart keys?

Yes, for most makes. Some newer models need extra steps or parts. Tell us the year, make and model and we’ll confirm before coming out.

Do I need to tow my car to you?

No. We bring the key machine, blanks and programmer to wherever the car is: home, work, a parking lot or the side of the road.

Can this damage my car?

We take care not to. We open doors with automotive lockout tools, not by force, and program keys with dealer-level equipment, usually through the car’s diagnostic port.

What are your hours?

We take calls and go out 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays.

How can I pay?

Cash, credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Zelle, Venmo or Cash App, whichever is easiest for you.

Can I text instead of calling?

Yes. Text (770) 270-3333 with the year, make and model and what happened. You’ll get a reply from the locksmith, not a call center.

Is there a discount on a second key?

Yes. When we make a new key because all keys are lost, a second key for the same car is 50% off. Same car, same key type, made during the same visit. Combines with $15 off for online booking.

How do I get the $15 discount?

Book your appointment through the online booking page. The discount is applied to online bookings.
