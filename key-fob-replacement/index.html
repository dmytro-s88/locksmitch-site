Common questions

How much does it cost to replace a key fob?

A replacement remote, supplied and programmed, starts from $129. Programming a remote you bought starts from $89. A flip key with a cut blade starts from $150.

My key fob stopped working. Do I need a new one?

Not always. A dead battery is the most common cause. If the battery is fine and the car still does not respond, the remote may need to be synced again or replaced.

The new key starts the car but the buttons do not work. Why?

The chip and the remote are programmed separately on many cars. Usually the remote step was not done, and it does not need a new key.

Can you program a remote I bought on Amazon or eBay?

Often yes, from $89, if it is the right part for your car. Send me the part number before you order. It is tested before you pay, but a part you bought is not under my warranty.

Is there a warranty?

1 year on keys and remotes I supply. Not covered: physical or water damage, lost keys and batteries.
