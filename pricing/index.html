What we do

Four jobs, all done at your car. Most are done within about an hour once we arrive.

All keys lost 2nd key 50% off

You have no working key at all.

We cut a new key from your vehicle’s code, program the transponder or smart key to the immobilizer, and leave you with a working key. Most makes and models, including push-to-start.

Spare key

You have one key and want a backup.

The cheapest moment to get a second key is while you still have the first. We clone or add a new key or fob so both work.

Locked out of car

Price questions

Why is the dealer more expensive?

The dealer needs the car on their lot, so you pay for a tow or a second trip, and dealer parts are priced at retail. We do the same cutting and programming at your car with the same manufacturer key data.

Is the starting price what I will pay?

For most cars, yes. The price goes up only for a newer model year, a rarer key, all keys lost or a long drive, and Mitch tells you the exact number by text before he leaves.

Do you charge a trip fee?

No. In Cumming and Forsyth County the price includes coming to you. Further out, the lockout and no-chip key prices step up as shown above; key jobs add $50 only beyond 25 miles.

What if the key turns out to be a different type?

Mitch checks the key at the car. If it is a different type than you thought, he tells you the new price before doing anything.

Do you really charge $75 for a lockout?

Yes, in Cumming and Forsyth County, day rate. Nights add $50. That is the whole price: coming out, opening the car, no damage.

How can I pay?

Cash, credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Zelle, Venmo or Cash App.
