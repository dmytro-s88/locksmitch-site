Quick answer

Make the spare while the key still works. A copy of a working key is cut and programmed at the car: with LocksMitch a chip key is from $140, a key with a remote from $150 and a push-to-start smart key from $180. Once the last key is lost, it becomes an all-keys-lost job, from $190 for most makes, and the car stays where it is until then. On a few cars, such as a Mercedes-Benz 2015 and newer or most 2020 and newer BMWs, losing the last key means the dealer.

Why is one key a risk?

In November about half of the calls to our phone line came from people with no key left, and several said it had been their only key. Keys get lost at work and in parking decks, they snap in half, and one even burned. With a second key at home, any of those is an annoyance. With one key, the car is stuck until a new key is made.

What changes when the last key is gone?

  • The price. A new key with nothing to copy starts at $190 for most makes, against $140 to $180 for a copy, and European makes are quoted by model.
  • The car can't move. We come to it, but until the key is made you are where the car is.
  • The paperwork. We check a photo ID plus the registration, title or insurance card before making any key.
  • Some cars go to the dealer. Mercedes-Benz 2015 and newer is a dealer job for us. On most 2020 and newer BMWs (BDC3) a key can be copied from a working one, but with no key left only the dealer can make it. More: which BMW key system your car has.

Why do used cars often come with one key?

Keys get lost between owners, and a car sold at auction or by a small lot often has one key or a key that only half works. Three of our own cases:

  • A 2012 BMW 128i Convertible, our own car bought at auction, came with a single smart key. We made a second one through the CAS module.
  • A 2026 Hyundai Tucson was bought used with two fobs, but the second one did not match the car. Hyundai and Kia keys are chosen by a part number that starts with 95440, looked up by the VIN, and a fob that looks the same can still be the wrong one.
  • A 2007 Honda Ridgeline had only the valet key left. We cut two full keys from it and programmed them.

If you just bought a car, count the working keys on day one: each must open the doors and start the engine.

Can you erase the previous owner's keys?

Yes. A key you don't have can be removed from the car's memory, so it can no longer open or start the car. With LocksMitch that is from $89, and free when we make a new key for you. See erase a lost key.

How is a spare made from one key?

We come to the car with the blanks, the cutting machine and the programmer. The blade is cut on the machine, and the new chip, remote or smart key is programmed to the car next to your working key, so both start it. Most cars take about an hour. We cut car keys ourselves: a hardware store is fine for house keys, but a car key with a chip or a smart key also has to be programmed to the car. More: where to copy a car key.

Where should I keep the spare?

At home or with someone you trust, not in the car. A spare in the glove box helps a thief more than it helps you. If two people drive the car, each should carry a key.

Common questions

Is it worth getting a second car key?

Yes. A copy of a working key costs less and takes less time than a new key after the last one is lost, and on a few cars, such as newer BMWs and Mercedes-Benz 2015 and newer, a lost last key means the dealer.

How much is a spare car key if I have one?

With LocksMitch: a chip key from $140, a key with a remote from $150 and a push-to-start smart key from $180 (2020 and newer from $200), cut and programmed at the car. BMW and Mini from $350.

What happens if I lose my only car key?

It becomes an all-keys-lost job: the lock is read, a new key is cut and programmed at the car, from $190 for most makes. The lost key is erased from the car's memory, free with a new key from us.

Can you make a key from a valet key?

Yes, on cars where the valet key starts the engine. We cut full keys from it and program them to the car, as on a 2007 Honda Ridgeline we did in Cumming.

I bought a used car with one key. What should I do?

Check that the key opens the doors and starts the car, then get a second key made from it. If a second fob came with the car but does not work, send us a photo: on Hyundai and Kia the key must match the part number for your VIN.

Can you erase the previous owner's keys?

Yes. Keys you don't have can be removed from the car's memory so they no longer start it: from $89, free when we make a new key for you.

Does the car have to be there?

Yes. A key with a chip or a smart key has to be programmed to the car, so we come to where it is parked: home, work or a parking lot.

What do I need to show for a spare key?

A photo ID plus the registration, title or insurance card for the car. We check this before making any key.

How long does a spare key take?

About an hour at the car for most makes. Spare keys are made Monday to Saturday.
