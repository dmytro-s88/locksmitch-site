Where the work happens

A dealer works on the car at the dealership. If you still have a key, you drive in. If you lost every key, the car has to be towed there first. A mobile locksmith comes to your driveway, office lot or the side of the road and does the work there.

How long it takes

Dealers often need an appointment, and some keys are ordered from the factory, which can take days. A mobile locksmith carries key blanks for common makes and usually finishes the same day, often within an hour or two of your call.

What it costs

Ask the dealer for an out-the-door price that includes the key, cutting, programming and any towing. Then compare it with a locksmith quote for the same key. At LocksMitch the starting prices are:

Original or aftermarket key

A dealer sells original keys. A locksmith can offer both: an original key, or a quality aftermarket key that works the same way and costs less. Ask which one you're being quoted.

When the dealer is the better choice

Some newer cars, mostly certain European models and some of the newest model years, use key systems that only the dealer can program right now. A good locksmith tells you that up front instead of taking the job. If your car is one of them, we'll say so and point you to the dealer.
